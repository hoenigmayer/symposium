01 — About

A Tradition of Reflection and crossing boarders

Since 2025, the Between the World Symposium has gathered thinkers, makers and researchers for two days of unhurried conversation in Vienna. One topic is chosen each year and examined from every angle — no parallel tracks, no noise.

One topic a year. Three days. Nothing rushed.

The symposium is deliberately small: 30 seats, a single lecture hall, and long breaks designed for the conversations in between. Past editions have examined the end of the rules based order (2025), and disruption:research (2026).

Between the Worlds Symposium is organised jointly by the University of California, Irvine (UCI), the Technical University of Vienna (TU Wien) and the Austrian Council for Research and Innovation (FORWIT).
